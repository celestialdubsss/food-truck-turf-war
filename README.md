# Food Truck Turf War

A 3D food truck battle game. Race your truck across the city to the grocery store, grab ingredients in two minutes, then drive to tonight's event, invent dishes, and out-cook the other trucks.

**Play:** https://celestialdubsss.github.io/food-truck-turf-war/

## Playing online with friends

1. Open the link, type your chef name, and pick **Host an online game**.
2. Share the 5-letter room code, or press **Copy invite link** and send it to your friends.
3. Friends open the link (or type the code under **Join a friend**).
4. Pick a truck. Each truck is a team of 2: two friends on one truck agree on the truck, colors, mascot and name together. A team with one player gets an AI partner.
5. When everyone has hopped in and pressed **Ready up**, the race starts. The host's AI rival teams fill any empty trucks.

Up to 8 players (4 teams of 2). The host keeps the game running, so the host should keep their tab open and in front.

On a 2-player team, the first player drives and the second rides along; in the store you each push your own cart from a shared budget; in the kitchen you share the grills and can take any job.

## Controls

| Where | Keys |
|---|---|
| Driving | W/S gas and brake, A/D steer, Shift boost, Space drift, H horn, R reset |
| Store | WASD move, mouse look, E grab, R put back, Q ping your partner, G give (AI partner) |
| Kitchen | A/D slide, mouse look, E take order / grab / place / serve, R knock off, Space cook, M swap a dish |
| Anywhere | N sound on/off, B announcer on/off |
